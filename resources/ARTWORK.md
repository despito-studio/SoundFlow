# Editorial artwork

Album/single covers in `covers/` were retrieved from the public Apple iTunes Search API for editorial music discovery cards. Artist names, collection titles, and original catalog links are preserved in `curated.json`. All cover artwork belongs to its respective copyright holders. Cards start a search; they do not imply a license to download the depicted recordings.

The native home screen displays artwork attached to real tracks in the listener's library and history. It does not use album covers as decorative mood collections. No generated mood artwork is shipped.
