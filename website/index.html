A few good answers.

Do I need to install it?

Choose the installer or portable archive. For portable, extract the entire archive and run the SoundFlow.exe launcher at its root.

Where does my library live?

Your library and settings are stored locally on your computer. Server sign-in is available separately; signing in does not imply automatic synchronization of your entire library.

What about macOS or mobile?

This release is available for Windows x64 only. Other platforms are not currently offered.
